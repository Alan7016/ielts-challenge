Eduardo Briceño explains why many people who work hard stop improving. He says that effective people switch between two zones: the learning zone, where the goal is to improve and mistakes are expected, and the performance zone, where the goal is to do something as well as possible with few mistakes. He argues that schools and workplaces often put people in the performance zone all the time, and he suggests creating “low-stakes islands” where people feel safe to experiment and get feedback.
