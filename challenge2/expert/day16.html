Speaking Parts 2 and 3

Based on the "Stepping back in time" speaking material.

Describe something old that you or your family own and that you feel is important.

You should say:

what the item is and what it looks like

where it came from

what it is/was used for

and explain why you feel the item is important.

Strategies for a strong Part 2 answer:

  • Give a full introduction ("Actually, there are a number of objects I could talk about, but...")
  • Pick the point you can say the most about first
  • Speculate about the item's origins if you're not entirely sure
  • Include a saying or quote someone once said about it
  • Say what the item is not, to add contrast
  • Compare the item to others you have
  • Make a concession ("I don't love it, but I'll always keep it")
