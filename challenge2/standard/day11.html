Grammar note — present perfect for personal experience

When talking about devices you own, the present perfect is useful for saying how long you've had something or what you've used it for: "I've had it for two years", "I've used it to take thousands of photos". Notice the difference from the past simple, which is for specific completed actions: "I bought it in 2022."
