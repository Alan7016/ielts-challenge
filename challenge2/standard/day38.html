Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone, while others think that students should pay for their own studies. This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion, which is that the cost should be shared.

On the one hand, those who support free university education argue that it gives everyone an equal chance. If students have to pay high fees, many talented young people from poorer families may decide not to study at all. For example, in Germany, where university is free, more students from low-income families go to university. Therefore, free education helps a country to use the talents of all its young people.

On the other hand, some people think that students should pay for their own studies. One reason is that graduates usually get better jobs and earn more money, so it is fair that they pay for the education that helped them. In addition, if university is free, taxpayers who did not go to university also have to pay for it, which seems unfair.

In my opinion, both views are partly right. I believe that students should pay part of the cost, but they should be able to pay it later, when they have a job. Students from very poor families should study for free. In this way, nobody would lose the chance to study.

In conclusion, although free education gives everyone a chance, I think the fairest system is one in which the cost is shared between the government and the students.

≈ 270 words · aiming at Band 6.0: clear position, both views explained, simple but accurate linking
