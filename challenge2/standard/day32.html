In this short talk, Matt Cutts explains how he stopped feeling stuck in a routine by trying something new for 30 days. He chose small challenges, such as taking a photo every day, cycling to work and writing a novel in one month. He found that the months became much more memorable, his confidence grew and he discovered that he could do things he had never imagined. Writing Task 2

Some people think that museums and wildlife parks should be free for everyone. Others believe that visitors should pay. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Write at least 250 words.

Write your essay. Try to finish in 40 minutes — press the button to start the timer. Leave an empty line between paragraphs.

Museums and wildlife parks are popular places to visit, but people disagree about whether visitors should pay to enter them. In this essay, I will discuss both views and explain why I think entry should be free or very cheap.

On the one hand, there are good reasons for free entry. Museums and wildlife parks are important places for education. Children can learn about history, science and animals in a way that is much more interesting than reading a textbook. If entry is free, families with little money can visit too, and everyone in society has the same chance to learn. In some countries, such as the United Kingdom, many national museums are free, and they are visited by millions of people every year.

On the other hand, some people argue that visitors should pay. Museums and parks are expensive to run. Animals need food and medical care, and old objects must be protected and repaired. If visitors do not pay, the money must come from taxes, which means that people who never visit also pay for them. Some people also believe that visitors value a place more when they have paid for it.

In my opinion, entry should be free, or at least very cheap, especially for children and students. The government can pay most of the costs, and visitors can give donations or buy things in the shop and café. In this way, these places can stay open to everyone and still have enough money.
