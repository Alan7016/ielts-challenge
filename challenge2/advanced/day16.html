Speaking Part 3

Question: What things attract tourists to a place?

Here's how Jamila answered the same question — notice she gives a general answer rather than talking about herself:

"Well, I think in general people like to go to places which are well-known tourist destinations because, generally, people like to feel safe when they're on holiday, especially when they travel to a foreign country. I think usually people choose places where there are plenty of hotels, so they can get good accommodation and plenty of things to do, so they don't get bored. Also, people tend to choose places where they think the weather will be good, especially if they want to do things outdoors."

Notice the generalising language: "in general", "generally", "people tend to", "usually" — try to use phrases like these in your own answer instead of talking only about yourself.
